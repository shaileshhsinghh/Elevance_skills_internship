Dynamic Surge Pricing for Delivery Fees This feature implements dynamic pricing for delivery charges depending
 on factors such as time of day, demand levels, and order volume within a specific region. During peak hours 
 such as lunch or dinner time, the system automatically increases the delivery fee using a predefined surge 
 multiplier. The backend continuously analyzes order activity and applies pricing rules to ensure efficient 
 delivery management during high demand periods. When a user initiates an order request, the backend calculates 
 the delivery fee through `/api/orders/calculate-delivery-fee` based on the current demand and time. Once the 
 user confirms the order through `/api/orders/create`, the calculated delivery charge is stored along with the 
 order details. Administrators can configure surge pricing rules, multipliers, and peak hour settings through 
 endpoints such as `/api/admin/surge-settings`. For demonstration and system configuration purposes, **the admin 
 login credentials should be included in the project report so that reviewers can access the administrative controls
  related to pricing configuration.
